What is research?
• A “contribution to the body of knowledge”
     • Who’s body of knowledge? – the current state of the art
     • How? Through the advancement through the development of novel datasets,
        algorithms, better results through improved performance and others…
     • How does anyone know?
            • Publication of manuscripts in peer-reviewed outlets
                  • “Peers” – experts in your field
                  • “Outlets” – conference proceedings (typically done first, allows for
                      gathering of in-person feedback, manuscripts are shorter)
                      followed by journals (typically done after conference publication
                      in a conference proceeding)

PROJECT FORMAT
The lectures will typically cover the core technical concepts. This will be followed by paper discussions involving role-playing inspired by Alec Jacobson
and Colin Raffel. We will be adopting a subset of the following roles:
•   Reviewer: The paper has been submitted to your conference/journal, and you have been assigned to review it.
•   Archaeologist: You’re an archeologist who must determine where this paper sits in the context of previous work. You must find and report on at
    least one older paper cited within the current paper that substantially influenced the current paper and at least one newer paper that cites this
    current paper. Look out for follow-up work that offers criticism of the current paper.
•   Quanta Correspondent: You want to write an article on the paper for Quanta. You must explain to a broad audience the main problem the paper is
    addressing, why it is important, and what the main technical contributions are.
•   Student Researcher: You are looking for a new research problem and the paper piqued your interest. You have to come up with follow-up project
    ideas based on the paper. These can be direct improvements of the paper, or projects that use the papers results as building blocks.
•   Reproducibility Checker: You want to check whether the claims of the paper are valid. You decide to create a small experiment (toy dataset, or toy
    model) and verify if the results hold. Make sure to check how robust they are to various choice of hyperparameters. (Will count in project demo)
   Fall 2026: every role also has an AI-augmented task and an audit duty (see next slide)

AI-AUGMENTED ROLES (FALL 2026)


   Rule: AI use is required, and every AI output your team relies on must be verified and logged in AI_AUDIT.md

   Reviewer: write your own review first (7 pts), then complete the AI Review Disagreement Table against the instructor’s AI-generated review (3 pts)

   Quanta Correspondent: your article vs. an AI-written article; blind class vote at the midway session (Nov 3)
