                              CS4371 Final Project (Fall 2026)
AI Use Policy (Fall 2026)

AI use in this project is required, not banned. Each role uses an approved AI assistant (e.g., ChatGPT,
Claude, or Copilot) as part of its tasks, and every AI output your team relies on must be verified by a
human and logged in the AI_AUDIT file in your GitHub repository (template provided). Unverified AI
content presented as your own work will be graded as incorrect.

For each AI output, AI_AUDIT records: the claim, its category (fabricated citation, nonexistent API or
function, misstated result, code that runs but is wrong, or overclaim), whether it survived verification,
and how you checked it. The completeness and honesty of this log is graded under Project Code.

                                          Project Proposal (20 points)                        (Due Oct 13)

Task 1: (10 pts) Reviewer role. First, write your own high-level overview (like a summary) of the key
contributions of the paper that your team has picked (7 pts). Then open the instructor-provided AI-
generated review of your paper and complete the AI Review Disagreement Table in the proposal template:
identify at least three specific points where the AI review is right, shallow, wrong, or fabricated, with
evidence from the paper (3 pts). Write your own review before reading the AI review. One page report
in pdf format, plus the disagreement table

Task 2: (5 pts) Describe which specific topic from the paper you are investigating in further detail over the
next two months and why you picked that topic? Describe how it ties to the topics that we have covered
in the class? Which application areas can the idea that you have chosen to be applied to? Appendix
(required): ask an approved AI assistant for three follow-up project ideas based on your paper. In the AI
Idea Appendix of the proposal template, label each idea as feasible, already published, or technically
flawed, with a one-sentence justification, and state whether your chosen topic came from your team or
from the AI.

Task 3: (5 pts) As part of the final presentation, you will be required to demonstrate software for a new
idea that you may have based on what you learned from the paper? For the project proposal phase,
describe which idea you will be picking and how you plan on approaching this task. Name the AI coding
assistant your team will use and describe how you will verify its output. Create your team’s AI_AUDIT.md
now; every AI output your team relies on must be logged and verified there.
